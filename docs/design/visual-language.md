# Visual Language (Stage 2) — chosen direction

> visual-designer artifact. Direction approved by founder from the light "creative" mockup
> (`lems_today_light_creative`). This governs color, type, motif, and mood for all screens and
> the design system.

## Direction: "Calm focus with earned warmth" — expressive variant
A warm, premium, uncluttered workspace that stays calm under load and puts the **launch
journey** at its heart. Opinionated and focused rather than a neutral task list. Light-first
(warm, inviting for evening sessions); a dark theme ships alongside.

## Signature motifs (what makes it *this* product, not a generic tracker)
1. **The road to launch** — the hero element. Launch readiness is shown as a *journey* with real
   milestone stops (brand → site → socials → content → sales team), not just a %. This is the
   emotional and navigational anchor.
2. **"Your one thing today"** — a single prominent focus card that picks the most important
   *real* (ship-it) task, with a Start button. Efficiency + anti-overwhelm + anti-tutorial-hell.
3. **Learn → apply ratio** — a quiet honest signal ("6 learned → 4 applied") that exposes
   tutorial-hell drift without gamification.
4. **Language as design** — "Learning is the fuel. Shipping is the distance." Copy carries the
   philosophy; warmth in the words, not in extra chrome.

## Color (light — primary)
- **Canvas** `#FBF9F5` (warm off-white) · **Card** `#FFFFFF` · **Soft surface** `#F3EFE8`
- **Text** primary `#221F1A` · secondary `#6E6860` · muted `#A39C90`
- **Hairline** `#EBE6DC` · **stronger** `#DED8CC`
- **Accent — forward / primary (violet)** `#5A43C9`; tint `#ECE9FB`; on-tint `#45329B`
- **Warmth — attention / deadlines (amber)** `#B9791A`; tint `#F7EBD4`; on-tint `#8A5A12`
- **Applied / shipped / success (green)** `#2E7D46`; tint `#E4F0E5`; on-tint `#245E36`
- **Rule:** violet = motion/primary, reserved and singular; amber = gentle attention (never red
  shaming); green = the reward color, earned by *applying/shipping* only.

## Color (dark — companion, to finalize in design system)
Warm near-black canvas (not pure black), same hues shifted for dark (violet ~#8F81E8 text,
amber ~#E0A64A, green ~#7Fc99b), warm-gray text. Tokens defined in the design system.

## Typography
- Sans throughout (Anthropic Sans / system sans). **Two weights: 400, 500.** No 600/700.
- Scale: screen title 20–21px/500; card title 16–17px/500; body 13–14px/400; meta 11–12px.
- Sentence case everywhere. Warm, plain, active copy (see content voice in design system).

## Shape, space, motion
- Radius: cards 14–18px, controls 8–9px, pills 999px. Generous padding, lots of breathing room.
- Flat: hairline borders, no drop shadows/gradients. Depth comes from spacing and surface tints.
- Motion: calm and quick (150–250ms, ease-out) for everyday actions; a slightly larger, warmer
  beat reserved for **Applied/shipped** moments. Full reduced-motion equivalents.

## Iconography
Tabler outline, thin and quiet. Icons support, never decorate loudly.

## What the visuals must never do (from the brief)
No clutter, no red shame states, no points/streaks/confetti-spam, nothing that celebrates mere
*watching*. Warmth and the biggest visual reward are reserved for real work shipped.
