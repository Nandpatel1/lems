We are a group of 3–4 friends who have decided to build a Marketing Agency together. Before we start acquiring clients, we want to become genuinely skilled in every major area required to run a successful agency. Instead of learning only the basics, our goal is to develop deep expertise in subjects like marketing, sales, branding, copywriting, lead generation, paid ads, SEO, content creation, client acquisition, communication, business operations, automation, AI tools, and everything else that contributes to building a world-class agency.

To achieve this, we'll be consuming a huge variety of learning resources—YouTube videos, blogs, documentation, online courses, podcasts, books, Twitter/X threads, LinkedIn posts, case studies, templates, and more.

The problem we're facing is that these resources are scattered across different people. For example:

* One friend discovers an excellent YouTube playlist on sales.
* Another finds a collection of SEO blogs.
* Someone else finds a course on Meta Ads.
* Another shares a useful AI workflow or marketing framework.

Right now, there is no centralized system to organize these resources, assign them to people, track progress, or ensure accountability. Because of this, resources get lost, learning becomes inconsistent, and deadlines are easily ignored.

We want to solve this by building our own web application using Next.js. This won't just be a resource library—it will function as an internal Learning & Execution Management System specifically designed for our team.

The core objectives of this platform are:

* Maintain a centralized knowledge base for all learning resources.
* Organize everything into structured topics and learning paths.
* Assign resources or tasks to team members.
* Set deadlines and priorities.
* Track progress and completion status.
* Create accountability so everyone consistently completes their assigned work.
* Reduce procrastination by introducing reminders, notifications, and visible progress tracking.
* Build a system that keeps everyone aligned and continuously learning together.

One important feature is accountability. Every learning item should behave like a task. If someone is assigned a video, article, or course module, it should have:

* An owner
* A deadline
* Progress tracking
* Completion status
* Notes or learnings
* Optional review by another teammate

Additionally, we want the platform to send reminders—either manually or automatically—through channels like WhatsApp, email, or in-app notifications. The idea is not just to remind people, but to create positive pressure and accountability so tasks are completed on time.

This is much more than a simple task manager. We envision it as an operating system for our team's learning journey before and during the process of building our agency. Over time, we can continue adding features that make collaboration, knowledge sharing, execution, and accountability even stronger.

Once you understand the context, I'll explain the workflow and processes I've already designed, and then we'll work together to architect and build the application step by step.
